# Elimination rounds and leads

Start the isolated server with `GAME_MODES=lms GAME_MAPS=forja MATCH_SECONDS=180 WARMUP_MIN_PLAYERS=2` and run the doctor. Join with two players or dev bots. Verify `elimination.kind` stays `waiting` with `startsAt` for the 30-second join window. Have a third player join and confirm readiness at 20 seconds. Check that all three are alive when round one starts at 30 seconds. With a WebGL client, check the join countdown in the match status. In `/status`, check `elimination.kind`, `number` and `alive`. After an elimination, check that the alive count stays down even after the normal respawn time. When one player remains, check that the round pauses, reports that player's ID and restarts after four seconds. With a WebGL client, verify the countdown beside the match timer (warning colour in the final ten seconds and hidden during pauses), and the lead banner and feed at the first outright score.

With a WebGL client, die in LMS while at least one bot is alive. Confirm the spectator bar and **aguardando a próxima rodada** appear through the pause or waiting window. Press Space to cycle through first person (including the followed weapon), both chase views, free camera and map overview. Use the arrows to switch between living targets. In free camera, click to lock the mouse and use WASD to fly, then press Esc to open the menu. Press J and confirm you stay in your player slot. When the next round starts, confirm your own first-person camera and weapon return and the spectator bar clears.

Restart with `GAME_MODES=gincana`. Check that eliminating one player leaves their surviving team in play and that eliminating the last player on that side awards exactly one round to the opposing team. Check the team alive counts in `/status`. With WebGL, check that the default follows a living teammate and switches to the other team when the last teammate dies. Use `1` and `2` to select either team.

Let a round reach its 90-second deadline. Check that the playing snapshot carries `el.endsAt` and that the pause snapshot omits it. Verify an LMS tie draws and that a Gincana team with more survivors scores once. With an idle timeout shorter than the round, keep survivors active and confirm dead players and late arrivals can wait past the idle timeout and return next round.

For a wire-level check, retain the snapshots and `lead` events. Verify that two successive lead events are at least 45 seconds apart in one match. Clean up with `down.sh`.
