# Remap match controls

Use a WebGL-capable browser and join a match. In the Esc menu, open **controles** and verify the list scrolls while the menu buttons stay accessible.

1. Change **avançar** with a real keyboard. Verify that Esc cancels a second capture and that the new key moves the player when the match resumes.
2. Change **mirar** to a middle or side mouse button. Check left, right, middle and both side buttons in the capture dialog. The context menu and browser history must stay closed.
3. Try an occupied shortcut and decline sharing. Verify that both actions keep their bindings. Accept sharing and verify that both rows show the shortcut.
4. Use **padrão** on a row, reload the page to check persistence, then use **restaurar todos**. Check the lobby and weapon viewer lists.
5. Open the radio and pick a line with a number. Verify that the same number still selects a weapon outside the radio.
